# Bar & gauntlet
A bar is named, fetchable, comparable, relevant. No bar → propose 2–3 with name / why comparable / what to measure / tradeoff, then wait.
Gauntlet A: fresh critic, labels stripped, A/B/TIE with cited evidence. Gauntlet B: weighted score.
Universal (0–10): user/commercial value 15 · correctness 15 · architecture/wiring 10 · usability 10 · security/privacy 10 · reliability/recovery 10 · evidence/test quality 10 · performance 5 · accessibility 5 · taste 5 · sovereignty/rollback 5. Floor: overall ≥ 8.5; correctness/security/reliability ≥ 9.0; critical failures 0; unsupported claims 0; rollback documented.
Voice sub-score (SYNTHIA): accent authenticity (3 native listeners, blind) 25 · TTFA P50 25 · prosody/naturalness 20 · pronunciation of brand terms 10 · interruption/queue behavior 10 · failure-path honesty 10. Floor: authenticity ≥ 4/5, TTFA ≤ 1.5 s live.
Web/visual: Collins weights in `../doctrine/collins-level.md`.
Loop: critic names the smallest decisive gap → route to stage → bounded repair → fresh evidence → compare again. Exit only on win, owner stop, safety block, or evidence that the bar itself is wrong.
