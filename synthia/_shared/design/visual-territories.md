# Three divergent visual territories (Collins Gate Four — owner picks one)

Governing constraint for all three: SYNTHIA is an *operating system* a Spanish-speaking operator trusts with money and decisions. Distinction before decoration; self-evident before experimental; nothing on screen without a field value or a decision behind it. Cliché blacklist in force (no purple-blue gradients, no glassmorphism, no decorative 3D spheres — ours must *mean* something, no fake metrics).

## A — Observatorio (restrained · recommended)
- Governing idea: an astronomical instrument. Nine bodies in orbit; you read the sky to know the state of the company.
- Grid: 12-col, wide margins; HUD text pinned to edges like instrument readouts.
- Type: `IBM Plex Sans` + `IBM Plex Mono` for numbers; Spanish diacritics tested at display sizes.
- Color: near-black `#07080c` field, sphere colors from `SPHERE_FREQUENCY_MAP` are the only saturation; approvals in slate → amber.
- Motion: only entrainment/phase; camera easing ≤ 600 ms; no idle animation when no meeting.
- Signature moment: when the council reaches coherence the orbits lock into one ring and the memo materializes as text.
- Risks: can feel cold; mitigate with warm es-MX voice and the CDMX Theater scenes for meetings.

## B — Kiosco (expressive)
- Governing idea: the council meets in real Mexico City places (Kiosco Morisco, Zócalo balcony) — the existing Theater3D lineage.
- Grid: editorial, asymmetric; place photography/3D as hero.
- Type: `Fraunces` display + `Inter` UI. Color: cantera stone, talavera blue, marigold.
- Motion: time-of-day lighting, location transitions.
- Signature: sunrise over the kiosk when a decision passes La Vigilante.
- Risks: heavier GPU; risks decoration over information; slower to read state.

## C — Tablero (experimental for an agent OS)
- Governing idea: a Bloomberg-grade operations board where the spheres are small live gauges in a dense grid; the 3D view is a drawer, not the home.
- Grid: 16-col dense; data tables are the hero; sphere ring is 240 px.
- Type: `Söhne`-class grotesk / `Geist`; tabular numerals mandatory.
- Color: white/blue terminal by day, dark by night; market colors by currency.
- Motion: value ticks and sparkline updates only.
- Signature: the “walk-away week” view — one screen shows decisions made, budget untouched, approvals waiting.
- Risks: loses the emotional identity the spheres give the brand.

Recommendation: **A (Observatorio)** for the OS shell, with Theater3D scenes retained as the meeting room inside it. Owner decides; do not average the three.
